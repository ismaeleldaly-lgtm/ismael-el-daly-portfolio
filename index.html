Solo Travelers Egypt

The Challenge

International solo travelers visiting Egypt often struggle to find a trusted community where they can connect, access reliable local information, and experience the country with confidence. Solo Travelers Egypt was created to bridge that gap through strategic branding, community building, and consistent marketing.

The Execution

Solo Travelers Egypt was built around a clear positioning: creating a trusted community for international solo travelers visiting Egypt. Every branding and marketing decision was designed to make travelers feel welcomed, informed, and connected while maintaining a consistent identity across every touchpoint. The brand was positioned specifically for international solo travelers rather than local travel communities. Its communication focused on transparency, authentic local experiences, and practical travel guidance, creating a clear positioning within the Egyptian travel market. Every element of the brand, from its identity and messaging to its community experience and content strategy, was designed to support a consistent and recognizable brand system.

The Outcome

700+International community members
4.7K+Organic Instagram audience

Yummy Food Festival

The Challenge

A food festival needed a complete brand identity, developed entirely remotely from a client brief, with no in-person collaboration. I was responsible for building the brand from strategy to execution, ensuring a consistent identity across every touchpoint.

The Execution

The brand needed to capture the excitement of the festival while establishing a professional identity that would resonate with both visitors and commercial partners. Drawing inspiration from the atmosphere of food, celebration, and the energy of the circus, I developed the brand identity from the ground up: color palette, typography, logo system, and a comprehensive set of brand guidelines to ensure consistency across every application. Once the identity was established, I extended it into social media content and video concepts, maintaining a cohesive visual language and brand experience across every touchpoint.

The Outcome

10.5K+Organic followers
30K+Audience views
50+New vendors joined

Marketing Strategy Plan · Dec 2024 → Jul 2025

Flagak

The Challenge

Flagak already had an established brand identity, but it lacked a structured marketing system. I developed a three-month marketing plan that transformed the brand into a consistent content and campaign engine, driving engagement while maintaining brand consistency.

The Execution

Flagak brings local Egyptian brands onto one marketplace app. The identity was already established, so the work here was the plan that made it show up consistently: a three month content calendar, then the content that ran on it. The calendar followed the moments that actually move an Egyptian audience rather than a generic posting rhythm: a February launch, a March stretch built around Ramadan under the theme "Grow Big During Ramadan," and an April phase, "Expand, Engage, Excel," through Sham el Nessim, Sinai Liberation Day, and into Labor Day. Every entry carried the same promise: 0% commission, 100% delivery, full support. Dozens of moments, one pitch, never diluted.
